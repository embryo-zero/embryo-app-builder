# Embryo App Builder

Embryo App Builder is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Appsmith](https://github.com/appsmithorg/appsmith) (v1.43-2074-gb78391474c, commit b78391474c). It does build your own dashboards and apps.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
